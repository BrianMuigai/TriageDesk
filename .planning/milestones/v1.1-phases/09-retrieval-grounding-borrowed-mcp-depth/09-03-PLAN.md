Wire the proven retrieval contract into the graph as an advisory, routing-neutral `ground` node that writes
its own `evidence` channel, prove it cannot influence the scored colour or the path taken, and record the
phase's design decisions.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC2 is a non-write claim. Retrieval must *show* the evidence that grounds a colour while
being structurally unable to *set* one — and the only way to prove that is to show the other channels are
byte-identical whether or not retrieval ran, and that no router consults the new channel.
Output: `_make_ground`, the `evidence` channel, the `score -> ground -> verify` wiring, `tests/test_graph_grounding.py`,
and the D21/D22 decision rows.

**CONTEXT decisions implemented:** D9-07, D9-08.

<assumption_delta_decision>
**A4 (`ground` re-running on the bounded `verify -> score` retry) — decided: accept and declare.** The
resolved placement is `score -> ground -> verify`, so a case that fails the deterministic check and retries
runs `ground` a second time. This is harmless and deterministic: the complaint is unchanged, so the same
`EvidenceResult` is produced, and `ground` writes only `evidence` (last-write-wins, no reducer needed). The
cost is a second node span and a second `retrieve_evidence` audit record on a retried case — only E-12
slices, and no test asserts `retrieve_evidence` multiplicity. This is stated in the node docstring, in the
`build_graph` docstring and here, rather than being left implicit. The alternative (placing `ground` on the
success arm) was rejected because it would leave a never-verified case without evidence.

**Evidence channel needs no reducer — confirmed.** Last-write-wins is correct because `ground` is the only
writer and its output is a pure function of the complaint.

**Router wording — decided.** The route functions must not name the evidence channel in their bodies or
docstrings; that is what makes the neutrality proof a real structural check rather than a tautology.
</assumption_delta_decision>
