<objective>
Land the retrieval capability's pure core and its published tool surface: one new pure module
(`core/retrieval.py`) that ranks a complaint into attributed, closed-set discriminator passages under a
contract that cannot express a colour, plus the seventh owned MCP tool `retrieve_evidence` and the
one-wave allowlist update that the project's set-equality contract makes mandatory.

Purpose: AGENT-03 requires that retrieval can *show* the evidence grounding a colour but can never *set*
one. Proving that at the contract level (before any graph wiring) is what makes the rest of the phase
safe: everything downstream consumes a type that has no colour field to write.
Output: `core/retrieval.py`, the `EvidencePassage`/`EvidenceResult` contracts, the public `tokenize`
owner, the `retrieve_evidence` tool, the four-site allowlist fan-out, and `tests/test_retrieval.py`.

**CONTEXT decisions implemented:** D9-01, D9-02, D9-03, D9-04, D9-05, D9-06, D9-09, D9-10.

complaint-addressable text, so a band passage would either emit a colour (forbidden by D9-03 and Phase-9 SC2)
or re-author band prose (forbidden by D9-01). Nothing is re-authored and no second reader is introduced; the
resolution is recorded in the `docs/DECISIONS.md` D21 row written by plan 09-03.

**No new dependency — confirmed.** No `uv add` belongs anywhere in this plan or this phase. If a task
appears to need `rank-bm25`, numpy, or an embedding stack, the task is wrong: the ranking is the in-house
integer scorer above.
