<objective>
Make the desk queue-shaped: an acuity-ordered waiting room the nurse can actually read, and a vitals form she
can drive from the keyboard.

Purpose: `store/borrowed.py:391` assigns `MAX(position)+1` and `read_queue_rows` (`:454`) orders by `position`,
i.e. approval order — so the queue reflects who was approved first, not who is sickest. `desk/views.py` renders
one case and never renders the queue, and the vitals form has `inputmode="numeric"` but no keyboard flow. This
plan adds the waiting-room panel ordered by acuity (then wait time, then the stable `position`), renders each
row's colour and wait timer, and makes the vitals entry keyboard-first.

Output: the ONE acuity rank in `core/rules.py`, `desk/waiting.py`, the `GET /waiting` route with an injectable
store seam, the waiting-room panel and its styles, the keyboard-first form, and the proofs.

**Safety argument.** The waiting room is a pure read projection: it calls `store.read_queue_rows()` and never
`upsert_queue_row`, `next_position` or any audit writer, and `desk/` still imports neither `store.queue` nor
`mcp.server` — so it cannot mutate a queue row and cannot reach `commit`. The order is presentation only: the
stored `position` and the idempotency key are untouched, so no approval, edit or retry changes identity. Every
rendered colour comes from a committed row and still carries its text label; no new path emits a colour, cited
or otherwise. The form changes are attributes only — no validation, no submission and no gate logic moves.
</objective>

## Safety argument (stated once, proven by the suite)

1. **Read-only by construction.** `desk/waiting.py` contains one store call (`read_queue_rows`) and no write
   method name; the module-level AST guard asserts it. The route catches a store failure and renders a panel.
2. **No identity change.** The plan never touches `store/queue.py` or the UPSERT: `position` and `created_at`
   are preserved on an edit, and the idempotency key is untouched. The order is a projection over the read.
3. **No new colour source.** The panel renders `row.colour` (a committed value) with its text label, using the
   ONE `_colour_class` mapping. It computes no colour and no rank literal.
4. **No new commit path.** `desk/`'s guarded import graph is unchanged; the package-wide AST guard stays green.
5. **Keyboard attributes cannot change a submission.** `autofocus`/`enterkeyhint` are presentation attributes;
   the form's fields, names, values and the schema validation are byte-identical.
