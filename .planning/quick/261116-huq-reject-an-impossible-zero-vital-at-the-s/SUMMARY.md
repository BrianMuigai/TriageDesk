## What changed

`src/triagedesk/mcp/server.py`

- Declared one module-level constant, `_ZERO_IS_NOT_RECORDED = ("respiratory_rate",
  "heart_rate", "systolic_bp")`, with a comment stating why: a zero is not a measurement, so
  the honest answer is "re-measure", not a band.
- In the **tool** `score_priority` (not the scorer), before scoring: any of those three fields
  recorded as `0` is dropped to `None` on a **copy** of the case (`case.model_copy`), so the
  scorer's existing `missing_vitals` abstain fires with its existing reason
  (`missing scored vitals: <field>; re-measure before scoring`) and no colour, total, target,
  citations or triggered rules are returned.
- `core/scorer.py`, the citation helper, the `@audited` interceptor and `data/*.yaml` are
  untouched. Any other legitimate falsy value (`trauma: false`) and any unscored column
  (`diastolic_bp: 0`) is scored exactly as before. The graph reaches this fix because its
  `score` node calls the `score_priority` tool (`graph/nodes.py:274`).
