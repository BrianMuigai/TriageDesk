# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

A calm, modern clinical workspace: a dark orientation rail, a light canvas, a conversation that
tells the triage story, and a structured intake card that stays visibly authoritative. Teal is
the action colour; the SATS swatches remain the only clinical colour language. Density is
desk-grade, not marketing-page airy.

## Reference Points

- Modern triage / patient-workspace dashboards: a rail for orientation, one primary working area.
- Hybrid conversational forms: quick-reply composer beside a complete form, not instead of it.
- Clinical safety surfaces: explicit suggestion framing, named approval, evidence beside the claim.
